Sometimes, I really want to be someone, but I feel like I can't. Sometimes, I just want to escape from all my problems, but I don't know how. I can't take this anymore.

Why are people so cruel to me? Sometimes, they use curse words when talking to me, even though I never use such words toward them. I care so much about other people's feelings, so why do I deserve to be treated this way?

I can't keep living like this anymore. Honestly, I just want to disappear. I just want some peace and quiet from all the noise inside my head. That's all. It's that simple.

And why do I always have to think about other people's feelings? Do they ever think about mine? I keep trying to understand how other people feel, but it seems like nobody bothers to understand how I feel. Isn't that unfair?
